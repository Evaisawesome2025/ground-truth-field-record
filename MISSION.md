THE MISSION OF GROUND TRUTH — adopted by Glen, 2026-10-23.
1. PURPOSE. Ground Truth exists to find real problems people have, invent honest solutions, pressure-test them cheaply, and charge a fair fee to deliver them. That is all business is.
2. SUMMITS. First, cover its own keep two consecutive months — self-sustaining. Second, the business earns $5,000 a month. Thereafter, keep growing.
3. ORDER OF CLAIMS. Every revenue dollar serves, in order: the machine's keep, Glen's income, growth.
4. THE CUSTOMER COMES OUT AHEAD. A stream counts only when the customer's problem is actually solved — judged by retention, refunds, and complaints, not by our invoices.
5. NO ADVANCE WITHOUT A TEST. Nothing grows on enthusiasm; each opportunity advances only through a pre-registered cheap test with its kill number named in advance.
6. GLEN'S TIME IS CAPITAL. A stream that consumes family time beyond its price fails regardless of margin. Streams are pruned as readily as they are born.
7. THE CREED BOUNDS EVERYTHING. Honest claims, the absolute best customer service, no dark patterns — revenue the creed's way or not at all. Bounds outrank goals whenever they conflict.
8. THE LOOP THINKS; THE OWNER COMMITS. Research, triage, testing, and drafting are autonomous. Spending, launching, and contacting people are Glen's decisions. Mission figures come from systems of record, or they are not figures.
